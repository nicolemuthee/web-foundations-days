About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for capturing short notes the moment they come to mind. It keeps your ideas in one list so you can find and review them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your idea into the "New note" field.
  3. Click "Add Note" to save it.
  4. Review your saved notes in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Live note count
  • Simple, distraction-free layout

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note
Tab Move to the next field
Shift + Tab Move to the previous field
